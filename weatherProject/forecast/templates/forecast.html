            How is this built, and what are its limits?
            • One model for the whole country. It learned from 16 places together; when whole cities were hidden from training it forecast them just as well.
            • Seven weather models, each weighed. Simply averaging more models did not help. Letting the model learn how far to trust each one did, a modest but reliable gain on rain.
            • A correction, not a rival to physics. Weather models do the simulation. Ours weighs them, fixes their habits and says how sure to be.
            • Honest testing. The scores come from days the model never saw. Accuracy alone would flatter any rain model, so we show how well the percentages match reality.
            • Limits. "Rain" means 2.5 mm or more in a day. "What happened" is a reanalysis estimate, not a rain gauge. Rain on one street can differ from the area average. Hourly rain, UV and air quality are the raw weather-service numbers, not ours.
            • Temperature ranges. The weather drifted between our training months and the newest ones, so ranges sized on the older months alone covered only 71% to 77% of the newer days. They are now widened to cover about 8 in 10 of those newer days. That tuning used the same newer days, so treat 8 in 10 as the aim, not a proven promise.
